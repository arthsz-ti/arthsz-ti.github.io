About me

Since March 2025 I've been an IT intern at a multinational automotive company. I develop, run and monitor attended and unattended RPA automations in UiPath Studio and Orchestrator, built on REFramework with exception handling and logging, covering the full lifecycle: analysis, development, testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), deployment and support. I also develop in Python (Flask, Django, Pandas) and support corporate systems such as SAP, PowerShare and BMC.

I built a Django web portal with role-based access control, now used by every plant in Brazil to centralize initiatives from any department, not just automation. HR, for example, can use it to handle employee benefits. The portal includes integrated AI and management controls. I also built an automation monitoring dashboard in Flask, integrated with UiPath Orchestrator through its API, and I'm working on a computer vision project to detect PPE usage.
